Our Offerings

PAATHSHAALA | Classroom Experiential Interventions

Just give us the good old whiteboard, flipcharts, props, some space and we're good to go! Say goodbye to dreary classroom sessions. Our sessions are designed to keep participants not just awake, but fully immersed.

We blend classic learning formats with powerful experiential techniques:

  • The magic lies in our detailed session plan, trainer notes, solid lesson plans, and a robust & train the trainer's model ensuring consistent delivery across facilitators and geographies.
  • We incorporate facilitation processes, storytelling, simulations, role plays, case studies, and reflective exercises to enhance the learning experience.
  • Our facilitators are trained to adapt in real time ensuring the learning stays relevant and responsive to the group.

Paathshaala is where structure meets spontaneity, and classrooms come alive.

BAATCHEET | Facilitative Interventions

Rooted in our IIED philosophy—Identify, Involve, Engage, Derive—these are not just facilitation sessions. They are deep, purposeful conversations that drive clarity, alignment, and ownership.

Whether it's our pure-process facilitators mapping the perfect dialogue flow, or our Certified FaciliTrainers blending content with process, we bring sessions to life like never before.

  • We create space for collective reflection and group wisdom harvesting, where insights emerge through shared dialogue and diverse perspectives.
  • Content are co-created with participants, enabling participants to learn from each other and build collective ownership of the outcomes.
  • Each session is an experience. Intense yet fun, reflective yet action-oriented ensuring participants leave with practical skills and powerful, lasting takeaways.

KHEL KUD | Outbound Learning

Ready to sweat it out and stretch your limits.

Our outbound learning journeys are perfect for leadership development, team building, strategy and planning, brainstorming and more! Whether it's foundational team activities or adrenaline-pumping adventure sports, our expert team crafts experiences that drive growth through action.

  • Carefully curated experiential challenges aligned to your business or team goals.
  • Expert facilitation that helps participants process the experience of the activity.
  • Structured debriefs that uncover blind spots, as real behaviors, mindsets, and traits surface and are reflected upon in a safe guided space.

This is not just fun in the sun.. it's skilling up through play.

LIKHA PADHI | Content Development

Whether it's technical content, SOPs, detailed processes, or heavy manuals, our wordsmiths are skilled at turning complex information into formats that are easy to understand, interesting, and conducive to learning. We simplify the complex so that your people can comprehend, remember, and use the information more easily.

  • Instructional design support for everything from modules to microlearning.
  • From interactive modules, bite-sized lessons, facilitator guides to clear, concise job aids and simple explainer content, we create everything ensuring your learners get what they need, the way they need it.
  • Not only creating the content but if you need support in delivering the content, we're equipped to do that too.

Whether you're launching a new process or simplifying existing workflows, Likha Padhi ensures your team gets the clarity and confidence they need.

PRAGATI | Organizational Development

Growth isn't accidental it's designed. At PRAGATI, we co-partner with organizations to drive strategy, strengthen culture, enhance performance achieving sustainable growth.

  • We use different modalities and blended approach to address both the "hard" (processes and structures) and soft (people, culture) aspects of an organisation.
  • We begin by deeper diagnostics to identify root challenges and have clear understanding of your needs and requirements.
  • We deliver customized solutions that align your strategies, structures, and processes to achieve meaningful and measurable outcomes.

We serve as your strategic partner, capability developer, and growth accelerator enabling you to advance with clarity, intent, and collaboration.

UNNATI | Assessment Development Centres

The first step towards growth is knowing one's strengths, improvement areas, and having a clear developmental roadmap. Our ADCs are customised to suit the organisation's & individual's needs.

  • Custom-designed competency frameworks and development tools aligned to your business goals.
  • A mix of behavioural simulations, psychometric assessment, case studies, and role-plays to assess in real time.
  • End-to-end support—from assessment design and delivery to individual development plans (IDPs). Not only offline but also online we bring the same depth, rigour, and experience to your screen as we do in the room.

Whether you're identifying high-potentials or preparing your next line of leaders, our holistic, data driven, human-centric approach ensures growth is not just measured it's nurtured.
